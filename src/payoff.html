How the numbers are worked out
  • The monthly payment is the same every month and pays the loan off over the plan’s years. Federal loans made on or after July 1, 2026 use the Tiered Standard plan: 10 years under $25,000, 15 years up to $50,000, 20 years up to $100,000, and 25 years above that. ELFI
  • Interest is figured each month on what is still owed. An extra payment lowers the balance right away, so less interest builds after it.
  • Rates shown are for federal loans made in 2026–27 (student 6.52%, Parent PLUS 9.07%). Each year’s loans get that year’s rate.
  • The 10%-of-pay line is a common guideline, not a rule.
  • These are planning estimates. The loan servicer has the official numbers.
